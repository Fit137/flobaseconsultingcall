# Dashboard 8 — Assets and Collaterals Library (Flobase)

Skill step 8.1. Three campaigns, three funnel stages each. The demand driver from Dashboard 1, the Agency Profit Leak Scorecard, is the TOFU asset in every channel, reformatted per channel.

**Channel fit note, read before budgeting.** The skill mandates LinkedIn Outreach, Cold Email and LinkedIn Ads, and all three are built in full below. For this ICP that ordering is wrong on one count: life insurance agency owners concentrate in Facebook groups, YouTube and IMO communities, and many are barely present on LinkedIn. Cold email is the strongest of the three because agency-owner email is obtainable and the CPA hook lands cold. Recommendation: run cold email at full weight, run LinkedIn at test weight against the narrow slice of owners who are active there, and add a fourth channel (Facebook groups and YouTube) as the primary paid and organic motion. The fourth channel is specified at the end of this document as an addendum, outside the skill's mandated three.

Assets 1 through 4 are inside Flobase's control this quarter. Asset 5 is not, which is why the design partner program in Dashboard 5 is on the critical path for marketing, not just for product.
