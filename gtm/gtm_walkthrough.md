# SLIDE 3, Competitive Market Analysis Dashboard

- Six competitors covering the four budget lines an agency owner already pays: Radius, AgencyBloc, Convoso, Ricochet360, Agent CRM, EverQuote.
- Flobase stands alone on four capability rows: lead supply inside the CRM, per agent budget control, CPA and ROI attribution, hierarchy and recruiting.

- So what: consolidation is a displacement motion, so every deal is funded by a contract the prospect cancels, which makes the acquisition argument arithmetic rather than preference.

# SLIDE 5, ICP Dashboard

- ICP Level 1, MVP to PMF: owner operated life telesales agencies, 5 to 25 producers, 500k to 3M in issued annual premium, 10k to 60k a month in lead spend.
- ICP Level 2, Growth: multi branch agencies and IMOs, 50 to 500 producers across sub agencies, 5M to 50M in issued annual premium.
- We win Level 1 first for three reasons: the buyer is one person, the pain is provable on the demo, and the incumbents price themselves out of it.

- Level 2 is reached through the product, through referral links and downline invites, not through an enterprise sales team.
- So what: the entry point is a segment every direct competitor has abandoned on price, which is the cheapest acquisition available to us.

# SLIDE 8, Lead Magnet, The Agency Profit Leak Scorecard

- Seven questions, under four minutes, mobile first, no email required to start. Built on ScoreApp.
- It returns a leak score out of 100, an estimated cost per issued policy, and a comparison against the roughly 500 dollar market cost per final expense close.
- It reveals the thing the owner cannot see: cost per issued policy per agent, and budget flowing to producers below breakeven.
- It converts because producing that number by hand means joining the lead invoice, the dial log and the policy record, which live in three systems that do not reconcile.
- Three result tiers route differently, Bleeding goes straight to a walkthrough, Leaking enters a four email sequence, Tight Ship gets the calculator.
- So what: the scorecard hands the prospect a management decision they want to act on this week, and acting on it means reallocating budget per agent, which is the wallet.

# SLIDE 10, Pricing and Packaging Dashboard

- Primary value metric: the active producing agent, an agent who spends wallet or submits a policy in the month. Recruits and dormant agents stay free, so unlimited seats stays honest.
- Secondary usage metric: wallet spend on leads and routed calls, at pass through cost plus a published platform fee, never a hidden take rate.
- Four tiers, Launch at zero, Agency at 299 dollars, Scale at 799 dollars, IMO custom. Agency is the hero and covers up to fifteen active producing agents.
- Expansion levers: crossing an agent band, the falling platform fee gradient from 12 to 6 to 3 percent, hierarchy depth when a second branch opens, and priority routing.
- ROI anchors: a comparable ten seat stack costs over 2,700 dollars a month against 299 dollars, and one IUL case at 6,000 dollars in first year premium pays for 1.7 years of the Agency plan.
- So what: publishing the price and the platform fee is the pricing expression of our positioning, in a category where Convoso, AgencyBloc and EverQuote all require a sales call before a buyer learns a number.
