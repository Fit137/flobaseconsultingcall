# Dashboard 4 — Ideal Customer Profile (Flobase)

Skill step 4.1. Two levels: the ICP that gets Flobase to product-market fit, and the ICP that scales it.

---

## Two-Level ICP

| Attribute | ICP Level 1 (MVP to PMF) | ICP Level 2 (Growth) |
|---|---|---|
| Segment | Owner-operated life insurance telesales agencies running a single virtual sales floor | Multi-branch agencies, IMOs and FMOs distributing leads and overrides across sub-agencies |
| Sub-industries | Final expense, mortgage protection, simplified-issue term. Secondary: IUL and annuity cross-sell inside the same book | All of Level 1 plus Medicare-adjacent life divisions, debt-free life and veteran-focused programs |
| Location | United States, concentrated in FL, TX, GA, NC, AZ, TN and UT where remote telesales agencies cluster | United States, nationwide, with recruiting reach into every state the downline is licensed in |
| Revenue, $ | $500k to $3M issued annual premium per year. Software and lead spend of $10k to $60k per month, most of it lead spend | $5M to $50M issued annual premium per year across branches. Lead and software spend of $60k to $500k per month |
| Team Size | 5 to 25 licensed producers plus 1 to 2 sales managers, all remote | 50 to 500 producers across 5 to 40 sub-agencies, plus branch owners, recruiters and an operations lead |
| Business Maturity | 1 to 4 years old. Past the founder-selling-alone stage, not yet running a finance function. Owner still personally approves lead spend | 4 years and older. Has an ops or finance hire, a recruiting engine, and carrier contracts held at the agency level |
| Business Model | Commission-only producers on advances, agency funds lead acquisition and keeps an override. Revenue is first-year commission plus renewals | Override and hierarchy economics. Revenue is branch overrides, lead resale margin and bonus-tier carrier compensation |

## Where to Start and Why

**Land in ICP Level 1.** Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. **The buyer is one person.** In a 5 to 25 agent agency the owner approves spend, owns the pain and implements the tool. That removes the buying committee from the first sale and compresses the cycle to a single call.
2. **The pain is measurable on the demo.** A Level 1 owner can produce last month's lead spend and last month's issued AP from memory. Flobase can rebuild those two numbers into a cost per issued policy live, which is the fastest path from problem to proof in this category.
3. **The incumbents price themselves out of it.** Ricochet360 enforces a $585 per month minimum, Convoso is not viable below roughly 20 agents, AgencyBloc requires a one-year term at $109 per user per month. A 12-agent agency is underserved by every direct competitor, which is a structurally undefended entry point.

**Expand into ICP Level 2 through the product, not the pipeline.** Referral links and downline invites mean a Level 1 agency that grows into a hierarchy expands the account by itself. Do not build an enterprise sales motion until at least `{TBD}` Level 1 accounts have organically crossed 30 seats.

**Explicitly out of scope for now:** P&C agencies, captive carrier agencies, Medicare-only shops, and single independent agents with no downline. The first three do not have the wallet and hierarchy problem in the same shape. The fourth cannot justify the platform fee and will anchor pricing too low.
