# Dashboard 6 — Value Proposition (Flobase)

Skill steps 6.1 and 6.2. One pillar table plus nine copy drills.

**Standing rule for this document:** Flobase currently publishes no testimonials, no customer names and no metrics. Every social-proof headline below is therefore written as a template with `{TBD}` slots. Do not run any of them until a real customer result fills the slot. Inventing the number would poison the one asset this positioning depends on, which is credibility about numbers.

---

## Brand Promise and Positioning

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| Brand Promise | Every dollar your agency spends on a lead, and every policy that dollar produced, in one place, attributable to one agent, visible the day it happens. Flobase promises an owner that they will never again have to assemble their own P&L from three vendors' exports |
| Positioning | Flobase is the only system that holds the agency's money, its leads, its calls, its people and its book in the same ledger. Others sell conversations, or the record, or the prospects. Only Flobase can tell an owner what a policy cost to issue and which agent issued it, because only Flobase owns every step between the budget and the book |

## Copy Governance Notes

- **Nine social proof headlines carry `{TBD}` slots.** They are the highest-converting variant in this category and the only ones Flobase currently cannot run. Filling them is the single highest-value output of the design partner program in Dashboard 5.
- **Do not run "The #1 All-In-One CRM for Life Insurance Agents" in paid channels** until a defensible basis for "#1" exists. It is a superiority claim, and this audience is regulated and litigious.
- **"Under 1 second" routing needs a measured, published number** before it appears in an ad. Buyers in this category have been burned by routing claims and will test it on the demo.
- **Body copy in every drill is at or under 25 words**, per the skill's constraint, so each is usable as-is in an ad or email without re-editing.
