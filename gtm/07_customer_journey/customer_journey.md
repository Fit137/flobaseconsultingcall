## Key Journey Insights

### Critical Success Factors
1. **The scorecard has to produce a number the owner did not already have.** If it returns generic advice, the entire top of funnel fails, because this audience has seen every generic insurance marketing lead magnet.
2. **The walkthrough must rebuild the prospect's own numbers, live.** A canned demo converts far worse here than a messy reconstruction of their actual last month.
3. **The funded starter wallet is the trial.** Nothing else proves routing, lead quality and attribution at once, and no amount of screenshots substitutes for a real routed call.
4. **First-month economics review must be a scheduled, staffed motion, not an email.** Value realization in this product is an interpretation event, not a usage event.

### Primary Journey Blockers
1. **No published pricing** stalls stage 3 harder than anything else, because the entire positioning is transparency and an unpublished price contradicts it on first contact.
2. **No named references** at stage 3. This audience buys on peer proof from Facebook groups, not from review sites, and there is currently nothing to point at.
3. **Migration fear at stage 4**, specifically the fear of losing selling days during a comp month. Unaddressed, it pushes every deal to "after this quarter" indefinitely.
4. **Absent commission processing** at stages 3 and 7. Agencies that need commission statements will keep AgencyBloc or their AMS, which weakens the single-tab claim and hands a competitor a foothold at renewal.

### Acceleration Opportunities
1. **Publish pricing and the wallet unit economics.** It removes the biggest stage 3 blocker and turns the category's most common complaint into the reason to choose Flobase.
2. **Compress stages 3 and 4 into one call.** The walkthrough and the funded wallet setup can happen in the same session, which is only possible because the trial needs no data migration to start.
3. **Turn stage 5 into stage 1 for the next buyer.** Every first-month economics review should end with a screenshot-ready result and an ask for a community post. This audience's discovery channel is other owners.
4. **Use the onboarding tracker as an expansion trigger, not a feature.** Every recruit entered is a future seat, so instrument recruit-entered as a leading indicator of account growth and staff CS against it.
