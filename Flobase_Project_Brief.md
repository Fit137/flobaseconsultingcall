# Flobase — Project Brief

Source: flobase.tech (homepage content only, as published)

## What It Is

Flobase is positioned as "The #1 All-In-One CRM for Life Insurance Agents." Its tagline is "Built by closers, for closers." The stated value proposition is: "Your entire agency in one tab. Take live inbound calls, buy and dial leads, drip SMS, submit sales, and track your book, all in one place." It is described as built for agency owners, consolidating team hierarchy management, lead generation/call routing, dialing, SMS outreach, sales logging, and performance analytics into a single platform.

## What Problems It Solves

Based only on language used on the site:

- Agencies currently juggle separate tools for CRM, dialing, lead marketplaces, and reporting — Flobase consolidates these into "one tab."
- Agents relying on cold leads face low intent — Flobase's inbound routing pitch states: "Stop chasing cold leads. Real prospects responding to insurance ads get routed straight to you, exclusive to your line, ready to talk coverage right now."
- Agency owners lack centralized visibility/control over agent spend — solved via the agency wallet ("Allocate balance to agents and control spend from one place," "no hidden fees").
- Onboarding and licensing progress for new recruits is hard to track — solved via the onboarding tracker (training course → licensing exam → contracting, plus bookable onboarding calls).
- Owners need to see organizational structure — solved via the live org graph and hierarchy table (agents, sub-agencies, admins, invites).
- Owners lack a unified view of sales and profitability — solved via the book of business, CPA/ROI reporting, and production heatmap.
- Slow call routing can lose high-intent prospects — addressed via "sub-second" routing, described as routing live calls "in under 1 second," to "the next available closer."

## Product Features (Explicitly Stated)

### Core Platform Modules
- Team & hierarchy management: single table for agents, sub-agencies, admins, invites; live org graph of branch structure.
- Onboarding tracker: tracks recruit progress from training course → licensing exam → contracting; bookable onboarding-call slots.
- Agency wallet: owners fund a central wallet and allocate budget to agents; real-time tracking of spend, cost-per-call, and balance; "no hidden fees."
- Live inbound & lead marketplace: agents take live inbound calls and purchase exclusive leads within the platform; framed as "the perfect mix to hit 100k AP in a single month."
- Book of business: centralized, searchable record of every submitted sale, policy, and client.
- Team performance dashboard: tracks spend, cost-per-acquisition (CPA), annual premium (AP), close rate, and ROI at agent and campaign level.

### Lead Generation & Call Routing
- High-intent inbound leads: consumer-initiated calls (from insurance ads) routed directly to agents; described as exclusive, never shared with competitors.
- Sub-second routing: live calls routed to "the next available closer" in under 1 second.
- Exclusive call assignment: each inbound call is exclusive to one agent per interaction.

### Agency Setup & Growth Tools
- Unlimited seats for adding agents.
- Role-based access control across the hierarchy.
- Referral links to support downline recruitment.

### Dialing & Outreach
- Zero-delay dialer for outbound calling.
- Drip SMS campaigns for lead nurturing.
- Integrated lead buying within the outbound workflow.

### Sales Tracking & Analytics
- Deal logging tied to the book of business.
- CPA and ROI reporting per campaign.
- Production heatmap for visualizing performance trends.

## Stated Workflows

**Inbound pipeline**: Client calls → 60–90 second qualification → close client → commission paid.

**Outbound pipeline**: Buy leads → SMS campaign → dial leads → close → commission paid.

## "How It Works" — Four-Step Journey (as described on site)

1. **Set up your agency** — invite agents, build downline, set permissions in minutes. Includes: unlimited seats, role-based access, referral links.
2. **Fund the agency wallet** — allocate balance to agents, control spend from one place. Includes: per-agent budgets, live spend tracking, no hidden fees.
3. **Start dialing and taking calls** — buy leads, load the dialer, route live inbound instantly. Includes: zero-delay dialer, live inbound routing, drip SMS.
4. **Submit sales and track ROI** — log deals, watch your book, see profit per campaign. Includes: book of business, CPA and ROI, production heatmap.
